About SpamGuard AI

SpamGuard AI is a demonstration of the power of edge computing and machine learning in the browser. By leveraging TensorFlow.js, we can run sophisticated deep learning models directly on your device.

Key Principles:

  • Privacy First: Your data is never uploaded, sent, or stored anywhere. All processing is done locally.
  • Accessibility: Works on any modern browser, on desktop or mobile, without needing powerful hardware.
  • Dual Models: Utilizes two distinct, expertly trained models—one for the nuances of SMS language and another for the structural patterns of malicious URLs.

Add to Your Device

For quick access, you can add SpamGuard AI to your home screen. This works best on mobile browsers that support Progressive Web Apps (PWAs).

On Mobile (Chrome/Safari):

Tap the 'Share' or 'Settings' icon in your browser and select 'Add to Home Screen'.
